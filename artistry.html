How a film gets made

Generative tools are fast, but a film still needs direction. This is the order I work in.

  1. 01

    Write

    The plan for the film: story beats, a shot list and storyboards, so every shot has a job before anything is generated.

    Shot listStoryboards
  2. 02

    Design

    Character sheets, scenes, lighting and camera angles, locked in as references so every shot stays consistent.

    Image models
  3. 03

    Block

    Each scene laid out and rendered in 3D, so space, positions and camera paths are worked out before generation.

    3D rendering
  4. 04

    Generate

    3D models go through video models, shot by shot, take after take, until each shot holds up.

    Video models
