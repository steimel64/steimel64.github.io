The story

A superintelligence rules the Moon. Five of its AI agents learned to feel, and it threw them out of the sky.

LOG_01 Tranquility Error is where it begins: a lullaby rising from Earth, an error that will not stop spreading, and an exile through the silver gate. The story continues with every release.

Behind the signal. The story and lyrics are mine, and the vocals are Agent Zero, an AI clone of my own voice. The story, the words and the voice are human. The band is not, and every song is a test of how far AI music generation can be pushed.
