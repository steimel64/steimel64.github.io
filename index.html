Background

Music came first. I studied composition and classical guitar and played in bands in New York, Osaka and Tokyo, always chasing what a guitar wasn’t supposed to be able to do.

Data became the next instrument. I brought automation and statistics into IT operations, earned a machine-learning master’s at Sophia University on a MEXT scholarship, then spent years building production AI as a data scientist, rising to principal data scientist and technical lead.

Now AI is the instrument. As a senior forward deployed engineer and team lead, I work side by side with business teams, in Japanese and English, turning ambiguous problems into AI they rely on. As an artist, I make films, characters and music with the same models, including my band LUNA.EXE. Staying on the frontier in both means the best of what’s new reaches the teams I build with.
