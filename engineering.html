Where I help

The problems teams usually bring me in for.

  1. 01

    Bilingual discovery

    I close the gap between Japanese stakeholders and the teams building for them, in both languages, and pin down what really needs solving before anything is built.

  2. 02

    Stalled prototypes

    A promising demo that never made it into daily use. I find what is missing, close the gaps and get it adopted.

  3. 03

    Agentic systems

    Agents a business can rely on, with real harnesses, evals and monitoring rather than a clever prompt.

  4. 04

    Data foundations

    The pipelines and platform an AI product stands on, built to keep working as usage grows.

  5. 05

    Interfaces people use

    When a system needs a front end, I design and build it around how people actually work, so the AI fits into their day instead of getting in the way.

  6. 06

    AI-driven delivery

    Teams that want to ship with coding agents safely, with guardrails to match the speed.
